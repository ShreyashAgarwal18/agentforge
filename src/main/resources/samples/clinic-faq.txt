Riverside Family Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions

Office Hours
We are open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on weekends.

Appointments
New patient appointments require a 30-minute slot. Returning patients can book 15-minute
follow-up visits. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete paperwork.

Services
We offer general check-ups, vaccinations, and basic lab work. For specialist referrals,
ask your doctor during your visit.

Insurance
We accept most major insurance plans. Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID to
your appointment. Self-pay patients receive a 10% discount.

Cancellations
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a $25 late cancellation fee.
