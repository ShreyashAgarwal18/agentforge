Sunny Side Cake Shop - Frequently Asked Questions

Store Hours
We are open Tuesday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays.

Signature Product
Our signature product is the Honey Lavender Layer Cake, priced at $42 for an 8-inch cake.

Delivery
We deliver within a 10-mile radius of our shop for a flat fee of $8. Orders must be placed
at least 24 hours in advance.

Returns and Refunds
Because our products are perishable, we do not accept returns. If your order arrives damaged,
contact us within 2 hours of delivery for a replacement or refund.

Payment
We accept cash, credit cards, and mobile payment apps (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
